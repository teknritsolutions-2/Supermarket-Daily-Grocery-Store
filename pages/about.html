About FreshAisle

A Neighborhood Supermarket Built Around Daily Kitchens

FreshAisle focuses on clear pack sizes, everyday staple availability, straightforward pricing, and practical tools that help households plan their weekly groceries without clutter.

Selection Approach

Fresh Produce & Everyday Staples Together

Our 48-product catalogue mirrors a practical daily grocery run: orchard and tropical fruit, staple cooking vegetables, chilled milk and paneer, unpolished dals, rice, whole-wheat atta, daily bakery loaves, and tea-time essentials.

Each item lists clear weights and pack options so you can choose a 500 g trial pack or a 5 kg kitchen refill with transparent pricing.

Grocery Standards

Handling, Storage & Practical Shopping

Produce & Leafy Greens

Crisp fruits and leafy vegetables are separated from heavy root produce like potatoes and onions to prevent bruising during packing and home storage.

Chilled Dairy & Eggs

Milk, curd, paneer, butter, cheese, and yogurt include clear kitchen storage notes so chilled items stay at proper refrigerator temperatures.

Dry Pantry Care

Grains, pulses, flours, and ground spices are organized by kitchen use and recommended to be kept in dry, airtight jars away from stove steam.

Digital Convenience

Shopping Lists, Windows & Order History

Instead of rebuilding your basket every week, FreshAisle lets you organize named shopping lists inside the Customer Dashboard, tick off items already in the kitchen, and merge the rest into one shared cart.

When reviewing delivery preferences, you can check neighborhood PIN-code coverage, pick a preferred time window, and record substitution instructions before placing your home delivery order.
